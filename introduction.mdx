NeverTooManyBooks is a free, open-source Android app built for anyone who has ever lost track of their reading list, accidentally bought a duplicate, or simply wanted a better way to manage their personal library. It solves the everyday problem of keeping a growing book and comic collection organised by letting you add titles in seconds — scan a barcode, search by ISBN, or type a title — and then automatically pulling rich metadata from more than twenty internet sources so you rarely have to fill in details by hand.

## Key Features

- **Barcode & ISBN scanning** — point your camera at any book barcode and the app does the rest
- **20+ data sources** — Amazon, Google Books, Open Library, ISFDB, Goodreads, BnF.fr, DNB, Douban, Bedetheque, and many more, each configurable and prioritisable
- **Bookshelves** — organise titles into as many named shelves as you like
- **Reading progress** — track what you have read, are reading, or want to read
- **Calibre sync** — synchronise your library with a [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) Content Server over your local network
- **Backup & restore** — full JSON-based backups keep your data safe across reinstalls and device changes
- **Multilingual UI** — the interface ships in 20 languages including Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), French, German, Spanish, Russian, and more, with additional translations maintained on Weblate
- **Tablet-optimised layouts** — dedicated two-panel layouts for 7″ and 10″ tablets, with graceful scaling down to 4″ phones

## Version & Compatibility

The current stable release is **7.16.1**. The app requires **Android 8.0 (API 26)** or later and has been tested through **Android 16 (API 36)**.

## History & License

In August 2018, NeverTooManyBooks was forked from *Book Catalogue 5.2.2* (© 2016 Philip Warner & Evan Leybourn). Since then it has been largely rewritten and substantially extended. The project is released under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)**, meaning the source code is fully auditable and freely redistributable.
